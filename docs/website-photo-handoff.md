# Real photography handoff

Eleven fixed slots: hero; Route 66, Dust Off and Final Mile package cards; two work/action photographs; business logo; two matching before/after pairs. No actual customer work or unrelated stock images have been published.

Joseph supplies originals and permission for visible people, vehicles and plates. In Owner -> Website photos, select the slot, supply alt text and a rights note, upload a draft, review it, explicitly confirm permission and publish. Replacements become drafts. Publication is audited; originals remain owner-only and only stripped JPEG previews become public. Intake/job photographs are never automatically published.

Use landscape hero/package/work shots, matching before/after framing and a clear square logo. Existing size/pixel limits and HEIC conversion apply. Real iPhone picker acceptance remains pending.
