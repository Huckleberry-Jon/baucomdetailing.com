Privacy policy

Updated October 28, 2026. Prelaunch policy — business contact details and operating terms await owner approval before publication.

Information used for your appointment

Baucom’s Mobile Detailing uses the contact details, vehicle information, service address, appointment preferences, notes and photographs you provide to review and manage your request. Doc Incorporated LLC provides the booking software. Do not upload identification documents, payment-card details or unrelated sensitive information.

Private records and photographs

Booking records and intake and job photographs are private. Authorized business owners can access them to manage service. A private booking link gives its holder access to that booking; keep it secure. Public website photographs require separate publication permission. A review is published only after your consent and moderation.

Service providers and communications

The prepared hosting uses Cloudflare for application, database and media storage. Email and phone notifications, when enabled, use configured delivery providers to communicate about appointments. External sending remains disabled in this prelaunch environment. Booking submission does not subscribe you to marketing.

Security, cookies and recovery

Essential cookies support owner authentication and private-preview access. No advertising trackers are included. Access controls and encrypted independent backups are prepared to protect records and recovery. Off-provider recovery has not yet been verified. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Requests and retention

Use your private booking page to cancel a request. For corrections, photo permission withdrawal or deletion requests, contact the business using its verified contact details once published. Business, legal and recovery requirements may affect deletion. A specific retention schedule and privacy contact must be approved before launch; automatic backup deletion is disabled.
