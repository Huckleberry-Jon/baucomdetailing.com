DocDetailing privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026. This policy describes the current DocDetailing private beta provided by Doc Incorporated LLC. The beta uses a private staging environment; testers should use synthetic records instead of real customer information.

Information the app processes

Owner account identifiers and authentication records are used to control access. Business records entered by an authorized owner can include customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, service addresses, vehicle information, appointment times, notes, job photographs, inventory, invoices, payment records, expenses and financial reports. Do not enter payment-card numbers, identification documents or unrelated sensitive information.

How information is used

Records support booking and scheduling, customer and vehicle management, job documentation, stock tracking, invoices, manual payment tracking, business reports, support, security and recovery. Owners must have authority to enter and use customer records. Business records are scoped to the authenticated business.

Photos and device permissions

Camera and photo-library access is requested when using photo features. Selected or captured job photographs are uploaded to private storage. Existing photos in your library are not deleted by the app. Invoice photos you choose to include become part of the shared invoice. Photo handling on actual iPhones remains part of beta acceptance testing.

Storage, access and service providers

Cloudflare provides the backend, database and object storage. Authenticated business owners can access their business records. Session credentials are stored using the iOS Keychain. Apple provides TestFlight distribution and any feedback or diagnostic information handled through TestFlight under Apple’s terms. Hosting providers may process request and security information to operate their services.

The current app has no advertising SDK, advertising tracking or sale of personal information. Records are not intentionally made public. A private booking link grants access to its booking; keep it confidential. Photos and documents shared through the iPhone share sheet are disclosed to the recipient or service you choose. Public website photos and reviews require their separate publication controls.

Communications and payments

Outbound push notifications and customer email are disabled in this beta. Notification-device registrations may be stored when you opt in; provider delivery will require separately approved testing. Integrated payment processing is disabled. Manual payment entries record amounts and payment methods, not payment-card credentials. Future enabled providers or materially different practices will require an updated policy.

Retention, recovery and deletion requests

There is no automatic deletion schedule in the current beta. Records are retained until removed through authorized maintenance or an approved deletion request. Recovery copies may retain records until those copies are retired. Independent encrypted backup and restoration procedures are prepared; off-machine backup storage is not activated. No system guarantees absolute security.

For access, correction, export, account closure or deletion requests, email huckleberryservices@yahoo.com with “DocDetailing privacy” in the subject. We will verify authority before acting. Requests involving a business’s customer records may require that business owner’s involvement; applicable recordkeeping obligations may affect deletion. Do not send passwords or customer records in your initial email.

Your choices and policy updates

You can sign out to remove the local session and manage camera, photo and notification permissions in iPhone Settings. Signing out does not delete server records. DocDetailing is a business tool, not a service directed to children. This page will be updated when the app’s data practices change. Contact Doc Incorporated LLC at the email above with privacy questions.
