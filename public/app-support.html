DocDetailing support

Support for the DocDetailing iPhone app and private beta, provided by Doc Incorporated LLC.

Contact support

Email huckleberryservices@yahoo.com. Include the app version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, what you expected and the steps that caused the problem. TestFlight testers can also use Send Beta Feedback. Do not include passwords, access codes, payment-card information or private customer details.

Signing in

This beta requires an authorized owner account and private-preview access code. Request access from your project contact. There is no public self-registration. Apple reviewers receive separate credentials and instructions through App Store Connect. Contact support for account recovery; do not post credentials in feedback.

Beta limitations

The website remains private. Use isolated test data. Outbound push/customer email and integrated payment processing are disabled. Business hours and service area must be approved in owner settings before appointment confirmation. Real iPhone photo selection, notification delivery and sharing are still subject to acceptance testing.

Records and privacy requests

For corrections, exports or account/data deletion, contact support with the type of request. Authority must be verified before records are changed. See the privacy policy for current data handling.

Troubleshooting

Check your connection and confirm that you are using the supplied staging account. Reopen the app and retry. If access has expired, sign in again. If you encounter a scheduling conflict, review the calendar before retrying. Preserve the error message and report it without including customer details.
